Hello!
I am a Ph.D. student in the [Combinatorial Optimization Lab](https://opt.yonsei.ac.kr/) at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, College of Computing, Yonsei University, in Seoul, South Korea, advised by [Prof. Hyung-Chan An](https://opt.yonsei.ac.kr/hyungchan/).
I expect to graduate in February 2027.
I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Yonsei University in February 2021.

My main research interests are approximation algorithms, online algorithms, and learning-augmented algorithms.
More broadly, I am interested in theoretical computer science and combinatorial optimization, and I enjoy learning about a wide range of topics.
I am always open to collaboration and happy to chat about research ideas!

I am a co-organizer of the [Yonsei CS Theory Student Group](https://yonsei-cs-theory-students.github.io/), where I help run reading groups on various topics in theoretical computer science.
I also co-organized the Workshop on Algorithm for Students (WAS) in [2025](https://sites.google.com/yonsei.ac.kr/was2025/) and [2026](https://sites.google.com/yonsei.ac.kr/was2026/).

I am currently doing research while fulfilling my mandatory military service in South Korea under the [Expert Research Personnel (전문연구요원) program](https://www.mma.go.kr/eng/contents.do?mc=mma0003719).
My service is expected to end in February 2028.
