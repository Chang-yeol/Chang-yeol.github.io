Hello! I am a Ph.D. student in the Combinatorial Optimization Lab at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, College of Computing, Yonsei University, in Seoul, South Korea, advised by Prof. Hyung-Chan An. I expect to graduate in February 2027. I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Yonsei University in February 2021.

My main research interests are approximation algorithms, online algorithms, and learning-augmented algorithms. More broadly, I am interested in theoretical computer science and combinatorial optimization, and I enjoy learning about a wide range of topics. I am always open to collaboration and happy to chat about research ideas!

I am a co-organizer of the Yonsei CS Theory Student Group, where I help run reading groups on various topics in theoretical computer science. I also co-organized the Workshop on Algorithm for Students (WAS) in 2025 and 2026.

I am currently doing research while fulfilling my mandatory military service in South Korea under the Expert Research Personnel (전문연구요원) program. My service is expected to end in February 2028.
